## Priority Issues

### [P1] Evidence is not consistently inspectable

**Why it matters:** The cards literally labeled Evidence carry the strongest career claims but expose no source, case study, resume reference, or explicit confidential-review boundary. Credential links lead to generic issuer information rather than personal verification. The claim-to-evidence positioning therefore breaks at its highest-value moment.

**Fix:** Give each material outcome one clear evidence affordance: source, case study, resume reference, personal badge, or a concise `available in confidential review` boundary. Keep one action per card.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`

### [P1] The strongest proof arrives below the first viewport

**Why it matters:** At 1440x900 the hero is 970px tall; at 390x844 it is 876px tall. Atmosphere, not evidence, owns the entire opening. Mobile Experience also begins with portrait material while the current role requires horizontal travel and a turn.

**Fix:** Reduce the ambient media footprint, reveal one verified outcome in the opening, and make the current role plus outcome immediately readable on mobile without a turn.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable layout`

### [P1] Mobile navigation and contact degrade at the conversion point

**Why it matters:** The mobile section strip exposes only 247px of 460px, has no overflow cue or active-section state, and uses 9.92px labels. The Experience step controls are 38.4px rather than the established 44px floor. Profile links compress five destinations, and Email requires Gmail instead of offering a universal route and recovery.

**Fix:** Add current-section and overflow signals; enlarge functional labels and rail targets; use a one- or two-column profile-links layout; provide `mailto:` plus a copyable address fallback.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable adapt`

### [P2] Interaction tax exceeds information value

**Why it matters:** Five section links, five profile records, six project stamps, Shuffle, horizontal role travel, and card turns make a hiring review feel like operating a collection. Key role identity disappears after turning, forcing memory work.

**Fix:** Feature the three strongest public projects and disclose the rest behind one `All projects` action; move Shuffle out of the primary path; keep role identity and outcome visible together.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable distill`

### [P2] The emotional arc does not close on a career action

**Why it matters:** The page peaks at Selected Outcomes and then ends on Credits & licenses. The rotating personal quotes and decorative artifacts add personality, but there is no final reassurance or clear route for a convinced reviewer.

**Fix:** Preserve the committed quotes but subordinate them to the evidence path. End with existing resume, research, LinkedIn, and email routes, without implying availability.

**Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`
