## Product Principles

The following principles are **inferred from repository evidence and pending owner
approval**:

1. Put evidence before assertion.
2. Serve a fast human review without removing the path to technical depth.
3. State project, privacy, ownership, and confidence boundaries explicitly.
4. Keep the public product deterministic, standalone, and inspectable.
5. Treat resilient and inclusive fallbacks as product behavior, not decoration.
