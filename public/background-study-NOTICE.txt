subject under a slow camera push, and the framing returns to its opening
position at 16.25 s: measured against blurred 96 x 54 samples, that frame
differs from the first by 0.353 mean absolute error, against an adjacent-frame
noise floor of 0.275 and a 1.090 difference at the source's own 64 s loop point.
The short cut therefore repeats more cleanly than the full-length file while
carrying a quarter of the data. Audio is dropped from both; the element is muted
and decorative.
