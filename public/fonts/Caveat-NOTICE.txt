Caveat-Note.woff2 is a modified subset. The upstream Caveat regular face was
reduced with pyftsubset to the 31 glyphs needed by the colleague's note on the
career rail, which is why the file is 15 KB rather than 110 KB. Because it is a
modified version it is served under the file name Caveat-Note rather than the
reserved name Caveat, and it is used only for that handwritten note and the
Learnt cards.
