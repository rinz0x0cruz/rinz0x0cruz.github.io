Kouzan Mouhitsu Font

Official source: https://opentype.jp/kouzanmouhitufont.htm
Calligraphy and font creation: Aoyagi Kouzan

The official source permits free use, unrestricted commercial use, use in
logos, publication in magazines and books, and inclusion on CDs. The font is
provided without warranty and is used at the user's own risk.

KouzanMouhitsu-Kanji.woff2 is a WOFF2 subset of the official OpenType font. It
contains only the nine characters used by this project and retains the source
font's metadata.
